Our Story

Rooted in the red soil of Goa.

TheDoodhCo began the way most good things do — quietly, in a small barn, with a handful of Jersey cows and a promise: milk should taste like the land it comes from.

Today, our family farm still runs on the same principles. Our caretakers know every cow by name. The feed is grown on our own fields. And every bottle we send out is milk we'd pour for our own children.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Do you deliver every day?

Yes — we deliver 365 days a year across most of Goa. You can pause or reschedule any day with a WhatsApp message the previous evening.

Is the milk pasteurised?

We offer both raw (kacha) and gently pasteurised milk. Let us know your preference when you place your first order.

How is it packaged?

Food-grade sealed bottles/pouches, chilled at source. We take empties back on the next delivery to keep waste down.

Do you take bulk orders for cafés or events?

Absolutely. See our Bulk & Subscribe section for rates, or drop us a WhatsApp / email with your quantity for a custom quote.

Are there any delivery charges?

Regular route deliveries within Goa are free for monthly subscribers. For addresses further out, a small delivery fee may apply depending on the distance from our farm — we'll always confirm it before your first order.

How do I pay?

Monthly billing on UPI or bank transfer. First delivery is COD if you'd prefer to try it first.
